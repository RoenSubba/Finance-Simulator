# Finance-Simulator
AP CSA final project
Description: A personal finance simulator that models the rela world impact of lifestyle creep, compound interest, and financial emergencies. 

Core features:
    Custom animation engine
    RPG career and education tree
    Dynamic life timeline
    Asset management

How to play:
    Ages 16-22: Focus on early investments, maintaining grades, and getting into college.
    Ages 23-50: Choose a career path, manage your fixed expenses (houses, cars, kids), and build your net worth. Avoid lifestyle creep!
    Ages 51-65: Maximize your retirement accounts before your 65th birthday to secure your final financial rank.
